# Learning Path

This file maps all 25 exercises across three tiers. Use it to find your entry point, plan a sequence, or assign exercises to learners.

---

## Tiers at a glance

| Tier | Focus | Exercises | Who it's for |
|------|-------|-----------|--------------|
| **Foundations** | AI as a thinking partner | 1–8 | New to AI-assisted work; building intuition |
| **Techniques** | Core prompting methods | 9–18 | Regular AI users wanting consistent, repeatable results |
| **Advanced** | 2025–2026 frontier methods | 19–25 | Frequent users seeking precision on high-stakes tasks |

Before starting any tier, read `docs/anti-patterns-2026.md` — knowing what *not* to do on modern models is as valuable as knowing what works.

## Suggested learning paths

### "I'm new to prompting" (~4 hours)
Exercises 1 → 4 → 9 → 12 → 13 → 23

Covers: AI as a tool, role-based perspective, basic techniques, iteration, and one quick-win advanced technique.

### "I use AI daily but want more precision" (~4 hours)
Exercises 13 → 15 → 17 → 18 → 22 → 24

Covers: systematic improvement, output evaluation, multi-path reasoning, principle-first thinking, and self-improving prompts.

### "I make high-stakes decisions with AI" (~3 hours)
Exercises 19 → 21 → 22 → 25, then read `docs/anti-patterns-2026.md`

Covers: strategic advisory framing, dependency-aware reasoning, confidence-weighted decisions, long-chain accuracy.

### "I want to build production-quality prompts" (full curriculum)
Follow all exercises in order. Read `docs/techniques-library.md` alongside each techniques exercise.

### "Just give me one technique that works immediately"
Exercise 23: Prompt Repetition — 20 minutes, no prerequisites, measurable accuracy gain on precision tasks.
